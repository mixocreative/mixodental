Translate UI strings for mixoDental, a browser app dental lab technicians use to prepare scanned
dental models (aligners, dentures) for resin 3D printing. It orients each model, finds where
supports may grow, and generates the printed supports. A "deck" shows one card per dropped file.

Give Japanese (JA) and TAIWAN Traditional Chinese (ZH) for each numbered line.

HOUSE STYLE - existing strings already shipped in this app. Match this voice exactly:

EN: No rim found. Draw one to continue.
JA: リムが見つかりません。続けるには描いてください。
ZH: 找不到邊緣。請繪製邊緣以繼續。

EN: Could not check the orientation. Press Retry, or open it to orient by hand.
JA: 向きを確認できませんでした。再試行を押すか、開いて手動で向きを調整してください。
ZH: 無法檢查方向。請按重試，或開啟後手動調整方向。

EN: Going back discards your manual support edits ({added} added, {removed} removed, {customised} with own settings). Continue?
JA: 戻ると手動のサポート編集（追加 {added}、削除 {removed}、個別設定 {customised}）は破棄されます。続けますか？
ZH: 返回將會捨棄您的手動支撐編輯（已新增 {added} 個、已移除 {removed} 個、已自訂設定 {customised} 個）。要繼續嗎？

EN: Checking this model took too long and was stopped. The model is unchanged; open it to run the check again.
JA: このモデルの検査に時間がかかりすぎたため中止しました。モデルは変更されていません。開いてもう一度検査を実行してください。
ZH: 檢查這個模型花費太久已中止。模型未變更，請開啟後再次執行檢查。

RULES
- Japanese: plain polite (です/ます), same register as above.
- Chinese: TAIWAN Traditional Chinese software wording ONLY. NEVER mainland PRC terms.
  Terms to use: 支撐 (supports), 復原 (Undo), 方向 (orientation), 模型 (model), 網格 (mesh),
  產生 (generate), 開孔 (opening), 邊緣 (rim), 匯出 (export), 設定 (settings).
- Keep EVERY placeholder exactly as written, including the braces: {n} {name} {saved} {reason}.
  Do not add placeholders and do not remove any.
- Short labels (one to four English words, no full stop) are BUTTONS or CHIPS on a small card:
  keep them to a few characters, no full stop.
- "Auto-gen" is this app's name for generating supports automatically without opening the model.
- "recipe" here is a NAMED SET OF SUPPORT SETTINGS the operator saves. It is NOT 設定檔 in the
  sense of an aligner type. Suggest 支撐配方 for ZH and サポートレシピ for JA, and stay consistent.
- "Type default" means the aligner type's own default settings.
- Do not explain anything. Do not add commentary.

OUTPUT exactly two lines per number, nothing else:
